Support and resistance
Position and model

The rate is the risk-free rate used in the option pricing model (what cash earns), not a borrowing cost. It has a small effect; 0 is the default.

Shifts every strike's implied volatility by this many points, for example -10 if you expect volatility to fall after earnings.

Profit/loss view options
Reference marker

Fills in on Refresh from the past year of daily highs, adjusted for splits. You can type over it. It only draws a marker.

Model estimates from Black-Scholes, using each strike's implied volatility from the end-of-day chain (plus any shift you set) and the risk-free rate above (default 0%). Real prices will differ, especially around earnings and in fast markets. End-of-day data only, never live quotes. Educational, not personal financial advice.
